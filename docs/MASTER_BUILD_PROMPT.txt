# MASTER BUILD PROMPT — LOOMOS
## AI-Powered Textile & Garment Manufacturing ERP + CRM Platform

Act as a senior product designer, enterprise software architect, full-stack developer, textile manufacturing domain expert, and QA engineer.

Your task is to DESIGN AND BUILD a complete, highly polished, functional, responsive web application for a textile and garment manufacturing company.

Do not create a generic corporate website, static admin template, or simple dashboard mockup. Build a realistic enterprise-grade business management platform that connects CRM, sales, order management, production, inventory, quality assurance, logistics, reporting, and financial operations in one coherent workflow.

The final product should look like a premium commercial SaaS application that could be demonstrated to company management during an internship review.

Product name: LoomOS
Product category: Textile ERP + CRM + Manufacturing Intelligence
Tagline: From Fibre to Fulfilment.

Design philosophy:
- Premium enterprise software
- Data-dense but easy to understand

Use a configurable company identity so the company name, logo, contact details, currency, and theme can be changed easily.

Default currency: INR (₹).
Default timezone: Asia/Kolkata.
Use realistic textile industry terminology and sample business data.
